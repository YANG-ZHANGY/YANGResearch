## Figure 1. Recorded legislative violence in Europe

Choropleth map of Europe showing the number of recorded legislative brawls by country or party-system episode. Countries with no recorded incidents are near white; higher counts are progressively darker red. Black country borders and bold numeric labels identify positive counts. The visible mapped cases are Ukraine, 19 incidents; Italy, 14; Turkey, 5; Moldova, North Macedonia, and Russia, 4 each; Albania, 3; Greece, 2; and Czechia, France, Montenegro, Serbia, Slovakia, and Slovenia, 1 each. These visible values sum to 61 incidents across 14 mapped cases. The complete country-count source file contains 65 incidents across 15 positive cases because Georgia has 4 incidents but is excluded by the map filter: the deposited Natural Earth geometry classifies Georgia outside the Europe subset. The colour bar ranges from light red for low counts to dark red for the maximum of 19.

## Figure 2. Predicted annual incidence across party-system closure and polarisation

Line chart of predicted annual legislative-violence incidents against party-system closure from 33 to 100. A solid navy curve represents lower polarisation at the 10th percentile, and a long-dashed burnt-orange curve represents higher polarisation at the 90th percentile. Semi-transparent bands around both curves show interval uncertainty, and a vertical grey dashed line marks the sample mean closure of 91.6. At closure 33, the lower-polarisation prediction is about 0.0012 incidents per year and the higher-polarisation prediction is about 0.0097. The higher-polarisation curve rises to roughly 0.0277 near closure 67 and then declines to about 0.0108 at closure 100. The lower-polarisation curve rises across most of the range, reaches roughly 0.0220 near closure 90, and is about 0.0200 at closure 100. The curves cross at approximately closure 84.4. The uncertainty bands are broad and overlap for much of the range, especially at lower closure values.

## Figure 3. Leave-one-country-out estimates for party institutionalisation

Forest plot showing 43 leave-one-country-out estimates of the coefficient for party institutionalisation. Each row omits the country identified by its ISO3 code. Blue points show coefficient estimates, horizontal blue segments show 95 percent confidence intervals, a red dashed vertical line marks the full-sample coefficient of −1.255, and a grey dashed vertical line marks zero. Every leave-one-out estimate is negative, and every confidence interval remains below zero. The least negative result occurs when Italy is omitted: −0.792, with a 95 percent confidence interval from −1.360 to −0.223. The most negative result occurs when Ukraine is omitted: −1.448, with a 95 percent confidence interval from −1.815 to −1.082. The estimates remain clustered around the full-sample value, indicating that the negative association is not driven by any single country.

Rows appear from top to bottom in this order: ITA, GEO, SRB, HUN, CYP, BEL, SVK, HRV, EST, ROU, PRT, LTU, ESP, FIN, CHE, DEU, GBR, DNK, SWE, MLT, IRL, ISL, AUT, NOR, LUX, LIE, SVN, NLD, CZE, AND, MNE, TUR, MKD, POL, SMR, BGR, ALB, GRC, LVA, FRA, MDA, RUS, and UKR.
